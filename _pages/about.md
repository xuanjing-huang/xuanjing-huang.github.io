I am Professor at the [College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence](http://www.fudan.edu.cn/en/), Fudan University, Shanghai, China, and hold a joint appointment at the [Institute of Trustworthy Embodied AI](https://teai.fudan.edu.cn/), Fudan University. I also serve as a Part-Time Advisor at [Shanghai Innovation Institute](https://www.sii.edu.cn/) and an Adjunct Advisor at [Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory](https://www.shlab.org.cn/).

My research interests are in artificial intelligence, natural language processing, and large language models, with a focus on foundation language models and reasoning, LLM value alignment, autonomous agents, and model interpretability. I have published over 300 papers in leading NLP, AI, and IR conferences and journals, with more than 45,000 citations according to Google Scholar. My work has received several awards, including Best Paper Awards at CCL 2019, CCL 2023, and the Instruction Workshop @ NeurIPS 2023; an Area Chair Favorite Award at COLING 2018; and Outstanding Paper Awards at ACL 2017, NLPCC 2019, and COLING 2022. Additionally, two of my papers have been recognized with annual outstanding paper awards by the Science China series journals.

In addition to my research, I actively contribute to the research community. I currently serve as an Executive Committee Member of the [Association for Computational Linguistics](https://www.aclweb.org/portal/about), Chair of [AACL](https://aaclnet.org/about/index.html), and a Steering Committee Member of [SIGIR-AP](http://www.sigir-ap.org/). I have also served as Program Co-Chair for [SIGIR-AP 2023](http://www.sigir-ap.org/sigir-ap-2023/), [EMNLP 2021](http://2021.emnlp.org), [CCL 2019](http://www.cips-cl.org/static/CCL2019/en/index.html), and [NLPCC 2017](http://tcci.ccf.org.cn/conference/2017/index.php), and as General Co-Chair for [NLPCC 2020](http://tcci.ccf.org.cn/conference/2020/).

## News

* **[2026.10]** The Fudan NLP group released a technical preview of A5 (v0.3.1, for macOS and Linux), a proactive personal assistant that gets to know you better the more you use it. It builds and revises a long-term understanding of the user and their projects through a Context index, a memory system, and a Workspace Wiki, tidied up during idle time by Autodream, and applies past preferences, experience, and decisions to new tasks. A Diff Engine notices changes in workspace materials and updates tasks already in progress, and a Proposal Engine suggests next steps before the user asks, acting only after the user confirms. The code will be open-sourced soon.
* **[2026.09]** With Shanghai Innovation Institute, King's College London, the University of Oxford and others, we released [SocioVerse2](/publication/socioverse2), an open-source research cockpit for social science. It turns social simulation from a cross-sectional survey into a longitudinal process that researchers can intervene in mid-run, forking counterfactual branches for comparison. Its population service draws on five persona pools, including X/Twitter and Xiaohongshu, with more than ten million indexed records, and its environment service connects 21 real-world signal sources. Seven case studies cover reproducing classic agent-based models, policy simulation, and macro index forecasting; over 32 months past the model's knowledge cutoff, simulated firms called the monthly direction of the PMI correctly 58.6% of the time, against 51.7% for the market consensus.
* **[2026.09]** We released [Atria Dawn](/publication/atria-dawn) with Shanghai AI Laboratory, an open-source (MIT) agentic model built on a 744B-parameter MoE foundation model with a 256K context window. It is designed for research and engineering work that needs tool use and multi-step execution, such as deep research, software building, and report generation. Across 16 benchmarks it is competitive with frontier agents and achieves the highest reported score on five.
* **[2026.09]** The Fudan AI course teaching team I lead received Fudan University's 2026 "Zhong Yang-style" Excellent Team award. The team has built 32 courses in the university's AI-BEST curriculum, one third of all courses approved.
* **[2026.04]** With Shanghai Qiji Zhifeng, we released [Agentic Harness Engineering (AHE)](/publication/agentic-harness-engineering), which lets a coding agent evolve its own harness automatically. Ten iterations lift Terminal-Bench 2 pass@1 from 69.7% to 77.0%, surpassing the human-designed Codex-CLI harness, and the evolved harness transfers to SWE-bench Verified and other model families.
* **[2026.02]** With Tencent Hunyuan, we released [CL-bench](/publication/cl-bench), a benchmark of 500 expert-crafted contexts and 1,899 tasks that tests whether language models can learn new knowledge from context. Ten frontier models solve only 17.2% of tasks on average, and the best, GPT-5.1, solves 23.7%.
