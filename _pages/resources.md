This page collects selected public resources from my recent work, including systems, models, benchmarks, datasets, and research platforms. For a complete list of papers, please see [Publications](/publications/).

## Agents and research tools

* [A5](https://huggingface.co/blog/KYLN24/introducing-a5) — a proactive personal assistant that builds a long-term understanding of the user and their workspaces, notices changes in project materials, and suggests next steps.
* [Atria Dawn](/publication/atria-dawn) — an open-source agentic model for research and engineering workflows, built on a 744B-parameter MoE foundation model with a 256K context window.
* [Agentic Harness Engineering (AHE)](/publication/agentic-harness-engineering) — a framework for letting a coding agent automatically evolve its own harness and transfer the evolved harness to new tasks and model families.

## Benchmarks, datasets, and platforms

* [WorkForge](/publication/workforge) — a framework for constructing verifiable long-horizon work-agent environments from real-world resources, with 16.7K environments across 40 professional domains.
* [CL-bench](/publication/cl-bench) — a benchmark for context learning, with 500 expert-crafted contexts, 1,899 tasks, and detailed verification rubrics.
* [SocioVerse2](/publication/socioverse2) — an open-source research cockpit for social science, supporting interventions, counterfactual branches, and long-horizon social simulation.
